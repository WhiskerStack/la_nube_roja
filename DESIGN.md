# Restaurant & Food Web Design Style Guide

## 1. Overall Aesthetic & Vibe
*   **Theme:** Premium, modern, appetizing, and immersive.
*   **Atmosphere:** High-end dining mixed with energetic fast-casual (depending on the specific section).
*   **Design Style:** Dark mode UI with high-contrast, mouth-watering food photography. Clean grid layouts with ample negative space to let the food shine.
*   **Feel:** Elegant, trustworthy, and highly conversion-focused (prominent CTAs).

## 2. Color Palette
*   **Primary Backgrounds:** 
    *   Deep Charcoal / Almost Black (`#111111` to `#1A1A1A`) for hero sections and main backgrounds.
    *   Warm Beige / Cream (`#F5EBDD`) for menu cards or lighter sections (as seen in the menu image).
*   **Accent Colors:** 
    *   Vibrant Orange / Amber (`#E67E22`, `#F39C12`) for primary buttons, highlights, and glowing effects.
    *   Golden Yellow for premium accents and text highlights.
*   **Typography Colors:** 
    *   Crisp White (`#FFFFFF`) for primary headings and body text on dark backgrounds.
    *   Light Grey (`#A0A0A0`) for secondary text and descriptions.
    *   Dark Brown (`#3E2723`) for text on light/beige menu backgrounds.

## 3. Typography
*   **Headings:** 
    *   **Serif / Script:** Elegant, flowing fonts for premium/romantic touches (e.g., "Cauh Lae Salh", "Passion in Every Plate").
    *   **Bold Sans-Serif:** Heavy, impactful fonts for fast food and energetic sections (e.g., "Delicious BURGERS", "Signature Collection").
*   **Body Text:** 
    *   Clean, highly legible sans-serif fonts (e.g., Inter, Roboto, Montserrat) for descriptions, prices, and navigation.

## 4. Layout & Structural Sections
*   **Navigation Bar:** 
    *   Transparent or dark background. 
    *   Logo on the left, centered links (Home, Menu, Specials, About Us, Contact), and right-aligned CTA (Order Online / Reserve Table).
*   **Hero Section:**
    *   Large, high-quality, cut-out images of food (centered or split-screen layout).
    *   Prominent headline with an eyebrow text (e.g., "EXCEPTIONAL CUISINE").
    *   Dual CTAs (Primary filled button + Secondary outlined button).
    *   Trust badges (e.g., "100% Fresh", "Award Winning").
*   **Featured/Menu Sections:**
    *   Card-based grid layout (3-4 columns).
    *   Each card features a large food image, title, short description, price, and an "Add (+)" button or cart icon.
    *   Hover effects (subtle zoom on images or glowing borders).
*   **About/Story Section:** 
    *   Split layout: Text on one side, overlapping/stacked images on the other (e.g., chef preparing food, restaurant interior).
    *   Statistics/Counters (e.g., "12+ Years of Excellence").
*   **Testimonials:** 
    *   Dark grey cards with customer avatars, 5-star ratings, and quotes. 
    *   Included social proof (e.g., "From 2,500+ happy diners").
*   **Promotional Banners:** 
    *   High-visibility blocks (often orange/yellow) with bold text, discount codes, and illustrations (e.g., delivery scooter).
*   **Footer:** 
    *   Multi-column layout (Quick Links, Contact Info, Newsletter subscription, Social Media icons, App Store links).
    *   Dark background with muted text.

## 5. UI Elements & Components
*   **Buttons:** 
    *   Pill-shaped or slightly rounded rectangles.
    *   Solid Orange/Yellow with white text for primary actions.
    *   Outline style for secondary actions.
*   **Icons:** 
    *   Clean, thin-line icons for UI (search, cart, user profile).
    *   Playful, colorful icons for food categories (burger, fries, pizza, drinks).
*   **Menu Tables (from Image 4):** 
    *   Structured with distinct headers for categories (Rice, Stew, Soups).
    *   Clear price alignments (e.g., 2L, 4L sizes).
    *   Integrated with circular food images to break up the text.

## 6. Imagery & Photography
*   **Style:** High-resolution, professionally lit, macro shots.
*   **Backgrounds:** Dark, moody backgrounds with textured surfaces (slate, wood) that make the food colors pop.
*   **Composition:** Close-ups showing textures (e.g., melting cheese, glazed meat, fresh herbs, steam).
*   **Effects:** Subtle glowing lights in the background (bokeh) to create depth and warmth.
